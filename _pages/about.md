I am a PhD student at the [Chair of International Accounting](https://wiwi.uni-paderborn.de/dep2/prof-sievers), where I work with Prof. Dr. Sönke Sievers. My research sits at the intersection of financial accounting and valuation — what drives long-term firm performance, how investors interpret accounting information, and how disclosure regulation shapes capital markets.

I teach in master-level courses on Mergers, Acquisitions and Divestitures and on Financial Statement Analysis and Business Valuation, and I supervise master theses.
